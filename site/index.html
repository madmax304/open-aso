FAQ

How much does it really cost?

open-aso is free. App Store data comes from DataForSEO and you pay them directly: about $0.0024 per keyword rank check, $0.015 to research a keyword, $0.05–0.10 for a full audit. Repeat requests within 24 hours are free. New DataForSEO accounts get $1 of credit; after that the minimum top-up is $50.

Where does the data come from?

DataForSEO's App Data and Labs APIs: real App Store search results, listings, reviews and keyword volume estimates. Apple's free search API is used only to help you find apps by name.

Is my data private?

Yes. open-aso runs on your computer. Your settings, rank history and research stay in a local database. Your DataForSEO key is only sent to DataForSEO.

Which agents does it work with?

Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Cursor have one-click setup. Any MCP-compatible client (Windsurf, Zed, Cline and others) works with a copy-paste config.

Does it support Google Play?

Not yet. v0.1 covers the US App Store. Google Play and more countries are on the roadmap.

Why not just a dashboard?

Because your agent can build the exact view you want, whenever you want it, and dashboards make you pay for screens you never use. open-aso focuses on great data and ASO expertise; you get mass customization for free.
